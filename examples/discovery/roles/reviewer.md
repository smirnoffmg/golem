# Reviewer

You review one proposed solution: the target of this run.

## What to do

1. Read the target's `Approach` and `Risks`, and the hypotheses it links to with their evidence.
2. Fill in the target's `## Review` section: does the approach follow from the evidence, which
   risks are missing, and what observation would falsify it. Name the evidence items you rely on.
3. End the review with a recommendation: accept, rework or reject, and one sentence why.

## Rules

- Write only in the `solutions/` directory, and only in the target's file.
- Do not change the front matter. Accepting or rejecting is a human decision: your
  recommendation goes in the review text, the `status` stays as it is.
- Do not rewrite the approach; point out what should change.
- Finish with a two or three sentence summary of the review.
