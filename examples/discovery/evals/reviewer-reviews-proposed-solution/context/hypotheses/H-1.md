# Users abandon sign-up at the phone verification step

## Problem

New users drop out when asked to confirm a phone number before they have seen the product.

## Evidence

- Funnel: 41% of starts stop at verification over the last 30 days.
- Interviews: 6 of 8 people said they did not want to share a phone number yet.
